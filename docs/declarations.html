Corollary 1 · sharp Serfling bound

Sample-mean and probability bounds

Theorem 2 · centered hypergeometric MGF

Best bound for the marked-item count

Proposition 1 · optimal hypergeometric constant

Exact value of the best constant

The literal maximum/supremum equals \(\kappa_N\): it is approached as \(t\to0\) for even \(N\) and attained at an explicit nonzero \(t\) for odd \(N\).

Structural reduction · binary and two-level

Internally verifies the structural reduction used by the manuscript: interval-valued populations reduce to endpoint values, and a maximizing centered weight vector can be chosen with at most two values.
